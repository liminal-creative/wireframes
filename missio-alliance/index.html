02.

What we heard

A project that starts from what churches already have

Here is our understanding of Familias Unidas from our two conversations and the grant narrative you shared. Let us know if we missed anything.

01

Rooted in six churches

A project of Missio Alliance, funded through Lilly Endowment's Christian Parenting and Caregiving Initiative. It helps parents and caregivers nurture their children's spiritual lives, starting with six partner churches. Resources come from the churches themselves, shaped by their own context and culture.

02

Latino churches first

Latino churches are the primary audience, and other churches are a second audience. The site leads in Spanish, with an easy switch to English for homes where parents and kids prefer different languages.

03

Warm, and easy to keep up

Its own warm, relational look, still connected to Missio's site (likely on a subdomain). It should feel alive but need little upkeep, so it can keep going after the grant ends. Live by mid-2027 would be ideal.

The site is how the churches' work reaches other congregations, in place of the podcasts and seminars in the original grant.

03 · Phase 1

What we'll create together

Phase 1 gives Familias Unidas its words, its look, and a home online. We'll start with the words, because the look and the site grow out of them.

01

Messaging foundation

A discovery session with you, building on the grant narrative you've already written, and a short messaging guide: what Familias Unidas is, who it's for, why it exists, and how it connects to Missio Alliance.

02

Visual identity

A logo or wordmark, a color palette and typography with the warm, earthy feel you described, and a simple brand guide your team and partners can use.

03

Website

A bilingual WordPress site on a Missio Alliance subdomain, Spanish first with a language switch, built so your team can add new content on its own.

04

Copy-editing

Edits on the site's page copy, and on the first case study and its guiding questions, so it can be a model for the others.

04 · What's included

A home for the stories and the resources

Language for the "why" behind the resources, so the site feels like more than a list of links.
