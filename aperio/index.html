01 — Introduction

Dear Aperio team,

We read your RFP a couple of times, and we spent a long time on your website as well.

What stood out to us is that everyone on your team has actually done the work of fundraising. You have been responsible for a portfolio, set goals, and felt what it's like when the money has to come in to meet the budget. Most firms that provide fundraising expertise don't have the kind of experience you have. And we get the sense that's why your partners trust you, and it is one of your key differentiators.

As we said, we read through your whole website, and we were still left wondering what you actually do. The thing that sets you apart is there, but it's not the thing anyone notices first.

We think it's important to figure that out before anything is redesigned. The following proposal is centered on two questions.

01What does your team say Aperio is?
02What do your partners say when a friend asks them about you?

The responses to those two questions are usually further apart than people expect.

Talking to Travis, we learned that your team refreshed its messaging and style guide this summer and trained your consultants on it. It's helpful to know that we wouldn't be starting from a blank page. We would test language you already believe in with what we learn through our stakeholder interviews.

He also told us to understand your goals: ot just more clients, but a national presence where someone hears Aperio and already trusts what you stand for.

You spend your days helping nonprofits make the case for their work. Being on the other side of that is more challenging than it seems.

We trust that this proposal connects what you are looking for with the expertise we offer.

We're looking forward to hearing from you.

The Liminal Team
02 — What We Noticed

Six observations.

These came from reading your site and your RFP. They are starting points, not conclusions. The assessment either confirms them or replaces them with what we learn from your team and your community.

01

Your website could be any firm's website

Proven approaches. Customized support. Every fundraising consultancy says something along those lines. But it doesn't communicate to a fundraiser what it is like to work with you.

02

The best thing about you is currently a footnote

Every consultant on your team has been in a development role, and you get your hands on the keys instead of handing over an SOP. Those are your two strongest differentiators. Currently, they live in a paragraph on the About page.

03

Nobody knows what Aperio means

We looked it up, but most people won't. We're sure there is something meaningful behind the Latin for "I open" or "I reveal." We'd love to explore this together.

04

The logo and the copy tell different stories

The mark shows leaves. The writing is about trust and partnership. The palette is calm, and parts of it likely fall short of accessibility standards on screen. Worth checking, since your clients are held to those standards too.

05

Fearless Fundraisers has more life in it

The sub-brand sounds like people. The parent brand sounds like a firm. It would be worth discovering which name your community actually uses.

06

One word we would test

Philanthropy is a word funders use. The people who hire you are fundraisers. We want to explore whether that word draws them in or keeps them at arm's length.

This is what we see from the outside. We'd love to get closer to learn things about Aperio we don't know yet.

03 — Our Approach

We work to get the words right first.

Every brand project we engage in starts with what is true, then we move on to how it looks, then to how it is implemented. An assessment is almost the entire first part. Skipping that important step is how organizations end up with a beautiful website that still doesn't clearly explain what they do.

PHASE 01

Listening

We converse with your team and the people who hire you. We survey your community, take a deep look at your competitors, and read everything you share with prospects. We will study the messaging guide you refreshed this summer, so we are measuring against your current language rather than an old version.

PHASE 02

Finding the through line

We turn what we heard into language your team resonates with. Who you are, what you do, and why it matters, written so everyone at Aperio can communicate it the same way.

PHASE 03

What you do next

We come back with what we would change and where we would start, covering your website, proposals, and how you describe your work. We will write a new About Us that you can use as soon as you approve it. You know your business better than anyone, so everything here is meant as a starting point for a conversation.

04 — Scope of Work

Exactly what we would do.

Your RFP listed seven things you want assessed, from your brand foundation to your sales materials. Below is what our work together would look like, step by step.

01

Kickoff strategy session

  • A 90-minute working session with your leadership team.
  • We get clarity on the goal behind this project.
  • We establish the interview list together and agree on who we will be talking to and why.
  • You provide all relevant content and materials, including the market research study and the messaging and style guide your team refreshed this summer.
02

Qualitative interviews

  • The questionnaires are developed by our team and approved by you before anyone is contacted.
  • Up to eight interviews over Zoom, one hour each. Five with your team and three with your community.
  • Aperio: Bianca, Mike, Travis, your VP of Client Services, and your SVP of Strategic Services. Travis named these five as the voices this needs.
  • Your community: current partners, past partners, trainees, and prospects who hired someone else.
  • Scheduling, reminders, and follow-up will be handled by our team.
  • We provide an interview report written by the person who facilitated the interviews.
  • We also ask how confident your team feels using the new language, since fluency and agreement are different challenges.
03

Community survey

  • A short survey for your partner base, concise enough that people will actually finish it.
  • The survey will test what we hear in the interviews alongside the larger group of stakeholders.
04

Peer assessment

  • Six to eight boutique fundraising consultancies that are going after the same work.
  • Where they overlap, and which claims have been repeated so often that they no longer register.
  • A visual and verbal comparison so you can see where you stand and what remains to be said.
  • How national firms signal local knowledge.
05

Search and AI visibility scan

  • What comes back when a development director asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Google for help finding a fundraising consultant.
  • Whether your site gives those tools anything to work with, and what to change so it does.
  • Good messaging no longer helps if machines cannot find it, read it, and repeat it.
06

Materials and channel review

  • Website, email, social, and print: read to see whether the messaging is consistent.
  • Proposals, decks, and case studies, read for whether they help you win new work.
  • Your events, trainings, and sponsorships, which shape the brand as much as the website does.
  • Your refreshed messaging guide and this summer's training, read for whether the new language is actually showing up across your channels.
07

Brand strategy

  • Mission, vision, pillars, and values, written out and signed off on by your team.
  • Voice, so your team writes as one firm rather than from a variety of perspectives.
  • A new About Us and boilerplate you can use everywhere as soon as it's approved.
  • Before and after, so you can see what changed and why.
05 — Deliverables

What you get.

A findings and recommendations report

  • What your team said, and what your community said.
  • The patterns in those answers, and what they mean for growth.
  • How you compare to your competitors, and which of your differences should be featured.
  • What to fix, in the order we recommend fixing it.
  • Where your channels and sales materials fall short, what it would cost to fix them, and what you would get back.
  • A plan for the next few months and a longer plan for a refresh.
  • Who approves brand decisions going forward, so it does not drift again in the future.
  • What people think of your new messaging, and what to adjust rather than replace.
  • How to sound close to a client when your team is spread across the country.
  • Who you should be talking to, based on where you do your best work.

Your brand strategy, written out

  • Mission, vision, pillars, values, and voice on a single page you can hand to anyone.
  • A new About Us and boilerplate, ready for your website and proposals.
  • The proof points behind each claim, so nobody has to invent them later.

We present everything to your team.
A document nobody refers to is a document nobody uses.
