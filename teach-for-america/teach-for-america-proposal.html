The summit is the only moment this year when 6,500 alumni are in one room.
03 — The Campaign

The Campaign

We would build a campaign that does two jobs at once. It gives alumni a way to express what they care about, and it uses the opportunity to show them what Teach For America has become through their responses.

The voting concept you sketched is a great instinct. Agency is something a single unrestricted fund cannot offer. We would sharpen it into something that holds up long after the summit concludes.

How it could work

  • Poll your alumni staff first to land on four or five initiative areas broad enough to fund real work.
  • Launch voting at the summit through a branded tap-to-pay activation in the main exhibit hall.
  • Continue voting online through the end of the calendar year, tied to your year-end push.
  • Keep every area open for directed giving on the site, and name the top vote-getter as the alumni fund.
  • Spend the spring telling the story of what alumni chose and what their gifts are funding.

Where we would push

  • Design the vote so no one feels their gift landed in the wrong place. Every area stays open, so every vote still counts.
  • Lead with participation. Your instinct to prefer 6,500 small gifts over one large one is the right one, and we would design to it.
  • Keep the initiative areas broad enough to feel like agency and flexible enough to function like unrestricted support.
  • Build a monthly giving path for recent corps members, where capacity is low and loyalty is high.
04 — Messaging First

Messaging First

We are a messaging-first agency. We do not design anything until we know what it is meant to say.

Your brand and messaging were built to recruit corps members. Inviting an alum to fund the work is a different job. Bridging those two is where this campaign will be won or lost, and it is where we would start.

Discovery

  • Interviews with 5 to 10 people, including regional fundraisers and alumni who already give.
  • Online forms for anyone too busy for a call, so calendars never hold up the work.
  • A close read of your new brand guidelines and the Power of Us summit tagline, so the campaign sits inside both.

What you get

  • A campaign name and theme grounded in what alumni actually said rather than what a conference room guessed.
  • Core campaign messaging, plus language for each initiative area.
  • Peer-voice story frames across the giving spectrum, from the teacher two years out to the alum now running a company.
  • A discovery draft you react to, then a final version once we know what resonated.
05 — Campaign Identity

Campaign Identity

Your new brand is well-made. We would not deviate from it.

In October, alumni will see the new look everywhere, on the website and across every surface of the summit. If the campaign looks like all of it, it will not feel like it belongs to them. We would align with the new brand and shift the balance just a bit so it stands out without breaking newly established guidelines.

What we will design

  • A campaign identity system that will pass your marketing team's brand review.
  • A standalone GoFundMe Pro landing page, using the templates you already have in the new branding.
  • Digital assets for regions, including handouts with QR codes they can print for local alumni events.
  • Email and social assets for the platforms your team uses.
Cohesive with the new brand. Distinct enough that an alum knows this one was designed with them in mind.
06 — Summit & Regions

Summit Launch and Regional Activation

October 2 and 3 are the dates we will work backward from.

At the summit

  • Design for the tap-to-donate and voting activation, built to spec.
  • A stewardship piece that alumni donors can wear or carry, so other attendees ask how to join them.

For your regions

Regional fundraisers will champion this if they are proud of it. Our job is to give them something they can be proud of.
07 — Momentum

Momentum Through June

A launch is not a campaign. Your fiscal year ends in June, and the months after the summit are when participation becomes an integral part of your donor base.

October through December

January through June
