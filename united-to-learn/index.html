05 — Scope of Work

Four phases, one narrative.

Phase 01 • Weeks 1–5

Discovery and research

  • 8 to 12 stakeholder interviews with senior leadership, the board chair, and, where possible, incoming leadership from the merging organization.
  • Interviews with your five program and service leads, for roughly 16 to 20 conversations total.
  • One to two focus groups with educators and community members.
  • Review of your strategic plan, existing messaging, and current communications materials.
Phase 02 • Weeks 5–11

Positioning and messaging

  • A brand messaging guide: positioning, narrative, value propositions, and proof points.
  • Merger rationale language written to hold up as details are finalized.
  • Audience-specific message maps for educators, investors, volunteers, and staff.
  • Anticipated questions and answers for leadership and staff conversations.
Phase 03 • Weeks 10–16

Templates and collateral

  • Internal announcement communications: staff email, talking points, and manager guidance.
  • External announcement materials: press release, partner and investor letters, newsletter copy.
  • Presentation and one-pager templates your team can reuse without us.
Phase 04 • January 2027 onward

Roll-out and brand management

  • A sequenced rollout plan tied to your announcement timing.
  • Hands-on drafting support through the announcement window.
  • Ongoing brand guardianship as merger details and organizational structure settle.
