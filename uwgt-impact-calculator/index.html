The people behind the numbers

Neighbors whose paycheck runs out before the month does

About one in three Triangle households cannot afford the basic cost of living. Around three-quarters of them are ALICE households (Asset Limited, Income Constrained, Employed), where someone is employed, and the paycheck still does not cover rent, food, childcare, and a car repair in the same month. One late bus can cost a person their job.

How is this number calculated?
  • The source. Every figure comes from 2025 end-of-year reporting by United Way of the Greater Triangle partners.
  • Monthly gifts are multiplied by twelve. The costs are annual, so a $25 monthly gift is $300 over a year, divided by the typical cost per person in each area.
  • Median, not average. Each area uses the median cost among partners working in it. A median describes a typical partner. An average lets one high-volume reporter set the number for everyone.
  • One area at a time. Costs vary by type of work, so results are never blended across areas.
  • We round down. Results are based on individuals, so the number is never overstated.
  • Donations are not restricted. Your gift supports our Community Impact Fund, our general grant fund for local nonprofit partners.
