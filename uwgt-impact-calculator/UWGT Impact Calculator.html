How is this number calculated?
  • Every figure comes from end-of-year reporting by United Way of the Greater Triangle grantees.
  • A monthly gift is multiplied by twelve, then divided by the typical cost per person in that area.
  • Each area uses the median cost among grantees working in it. A median describes a typical partner. An average lets one high-volume reporter set the number for everyone.
  • Results round down to whole people, so the number is never overstated.
  • Costs vary by type of work, so results are never blended across areas.
