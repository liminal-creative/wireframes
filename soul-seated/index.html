01 — Introduction

Dear Tiffany, Jonathan, and the Soul Seated Team,

Thank you for the opportunity to be in conversation with you about this project. What really stood out to me on our call is that Soul Seated is not missing material. You have a foundational philosophy, a theory of change, a brand expression doctrine, proposed copy for every page, and a photo and video library from the sanctuaries. The pieces are all there.

What is missing is putting it all together in a way that will be cohesive and compelling to your audience. You named it... you need a "through line," and there needs to be a clear message every time someone asks what Soul Seated is. That through line will give shape to your language internally and will increase your credibility externally.

Soul Seated needs to carry weight with academic partners, institutional funders, practitioners, and advisors, all while holding the character of the work rather than flattening it into another nonprofit program page. Our job is to stay close enough to understand you and at enough of a distance to make sure your work connects with people who aren't in it every day like you are.

What you will see below is a focused eight-week plan to have the new Soul Seated website live before your end-of-September launch. We hope to partner with you to make that happen.

With gratitude,
Bill & the Liminal Team

03 — What We'll Do Together

What We'll Do Together

Outside-In Review and Listening

  • We read everything you have built, from the foundational philosophy and theory of change to the brand guideline, the expression doctrine, and your proposed page copy.
  • We listen to Tiffany, Jonathan, and Ali, along with a small set of advisors and partners who see the work from outside.
  • Then we reflect back what an outsider actually hears, so you can say "that's it" rather than start over.

Brand Messaging for the Parent Brand

  • We define the core message set for Soul Seated, from why you exist to the words that carry it.
  • We sharpen how you sit apart from a wellness field centered on the individual, and give the contribution beyond the self its own plain language.
  • We build it around our Four Spaces of Engagement so every audience gets the right amount of story at the right moment.

Visual Language Direction

  • We let the message drive the mood, then extend your brand guideline into a visual system that holds handcrafted warmth alongside youthful energy.
  • We define how Soul Seated and Soul Seated Journey read as one family, so a visitor moving between them knows where they are.
  • You receive a short direction addendum for your team, not a full identity rebuild.

Website Design and Build

  • We design six to eight pages in Figma, using your storyboard flow and proposed copy as raw material rather than a blank page.
  • We build the site on a flexible page builder your team can edit, and put your sanctuary photography and video to work throughout.
  • Pages include home, why we exist, our model, early evidence, stewardship and team, and work with us.

Launch and Handoff

  • We train your team on editing, then launch by September 30 with room for the site to keep unfolding.
  • You get a short punch list of what to add first once the immersion program is underway.

04 — Four Spaces of Engagement

How Partners Move Closer

Most organizations tell the kitchen table story on the sidewalk, and people disengage because it is too much to hold. This framework maps how much story each moment can carry.

01

Sidewalk

Twelve words. A funder or partner hears what Soul Seated is and decides whether to lean in.

02

Front Porch

A closer look at the worldview, the gap you fill, and why this work is different from wellness as usual.

03

Living Room

The model, the early evidence, and the stories behind the numbers. An honest invitation to participate.

04

Kitchen Table

Advisors, institutional partners, and committed funders who help carry the work forward.
